Privacy Policy

Roast My Browsing History — Last updated: June 15, 2025

Overview

Roast My Browsing History is a Chrome extension that analyzes your local browser history to generate a humorous, personalized report about your browsing habits. All processing happens entirely on your device. No data is collected, transmitted, or stored by us at any point.

What data is accessed

The extension requests the history browser permission in order to read your local browsing history using Chrome's built-in chrome.history API. Specifically, it reads:

  • URLs and page titles of recently visited pages
  • Visit timestamps and visit counts
  • Navigation transition types (e.g. typed, link, reload)

This data is used solely to compute on-device metrics — such as top visited domains, peak browsing hours, session lengths, and search query topics — that power the roast report displayed to you.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not transmit any browsing history or derived data to any server.
  • We do not store any data beyond the current browser session.
  • We do not use cookies, local storage, or any other persistence mechanism.
  • We do not include any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • We do not share any information with third parties.

Data retention

No data is retained. Every time you open the extension, it reads your history fresh, computes the report in memory, and displays it. When you close the popup or report tab, all computed data is discarded.

Third-party services

This extension does not communicate with any external services, APIs, or servers. It operates fully offline after installation.

Children's privacy

This extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a future version of the extension, the updated policy will be published at this URL and the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
